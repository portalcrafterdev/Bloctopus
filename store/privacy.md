## The short version

Blocktopus does not collect, transmit, or share any information about you. It
has no network access and no analytics.

## What the app stores

Blocktopus saves one file on your own device. It holds:

- which level you have reached, and the stars earned on each level
- how many of each booster you have
- your sound, music, haptics and reduce motion settings
- your total score and the number of levels completed

This file never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Removing the app removes
it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not make network requests of any kind.
- It does not contain advertising or advertising identifiers.
- It does not use analytics or crash reporting services.
- It does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
- It does not have accounts, sign-in, or purchases.
- It does not track you across apps or websites.

## Children

Blocktopus is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from
anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before
that version is released, and the change will be described in the release
notes.
